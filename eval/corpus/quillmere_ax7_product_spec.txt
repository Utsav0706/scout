Quillmere Robotics Ltd - AX-7 Autonomous Picking Robot - Product Specification
Revision C, January 2025. Product manager: Elif Durmaz.

1. Overview
The AX-7 is an autonomous mobile robot that picks items from warehouse shelving and carries them to packing stations. It went on general sale in April 2025. Every AX-7 is assembled at Quillmere's plant in Coventry, England.

2. Handling
- Maximum payload: 35 kg.
- Maximum shelf reach (height of the highest pick point): 2.4 m.
- Gripper: dual vacuum and two-finger mechanical gripper, changed automatically.

3. Movement and navigation
- Maximum speed: 2.0 m/s in robot-only zones.
- Speed limit in zones shared with people: 1.2 m/s.
- Navigation: 360-degree LiDAR plus ceiling-mounted fiducial markers.
- Positioning accuracy: plus or minus 10 mm.

4. Power
- Battery: 48 V lithium iron phosphate (LiFePO4) pack, 2.4 kWh capacity.
- Typical runtime: 9 hours per charge.
- Fast charging: 0 to 80 percent in 55 minutes at a Quillmere charging dock.
- Battery warranty: at least 70 percent of original capacity after 3,000 charge cycles.

5. Safety
- Certified to ISO 3691-4.
- Performance level PL d for personnel detection and stopping.
- Safety watchdog: the robot stops if it receives no heartbeat from the fleet controller for 5 seconds.
- Physical emergency stop buttons on all four sides.

6. Physical
- Weight: 182 kg including battery.
- Footprint: 780 mm x 620 mm.
- Ingress protection: IP54.
- Operating temperature: 0 to 40 degrees Celsius.

7. Commercial
- List price: GBP 41,500 per robot (UK, excluding VAT).
- Fleet software licence (Conductor): GBP 2,100 per robot per year.
- Warranty: 3 years parts and labour.
- Minimum order: 10 robots for a new site.
