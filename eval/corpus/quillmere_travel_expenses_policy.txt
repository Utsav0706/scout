Quillmere Robotics Ltd - Travel and Expenses Policy
Version 4.2, effective 1 March 2025. Policy owner: Priya Okafor, Finance Director.
FICTIONAL DOCUMENT created for the Scout evaluation set. Quillmere Robotics is not a real company.

1. Purpose and scope
This policy covers business travel and expenses for all Quillmere Robotics employees and contractors. It replaces version 4.1. Spending company money should be treated with the same care as spending your own.

2. Approval before travel
Any trip with a total expected cost above GBP 1,500 must be approved in advance by the traveller's line manager and by the Finance team.
All international trips, whatever the cost, must also be approved in advance by a Director.
Trips booked without the required approval may not be reimbursed.

3. Rail travel
Standard class is the default for all rail journeys.
First class is allowed only for single journeys scheduled to take more than 3 hours.
Book rail tickets at least 7 days ahead where possible to get advance fares.

4. Air travel
Economy class must be used for flights with a scheduled duration of 6 hours or less.
Premium economy may be used for flights scheduled to take more than 6 hours.
Business class is never reimbursed, except for members of the Board.

5. Hotels
The maximum reimbursable room rate per night, excluding breakfast, is:
- London: GBP 190
- Other UK cities: GBP 120
- Germany: EUR 160
- United States: USD 250
- Any other country: ask Finance before booking.
Rooms above these caps need written approval from the Finance Director before booking.

6. Meals
The meal allowance is GBP 35 per day for travel within the UK.
Itemised receipts are required for any single item above GBP 10.
Alcohol is not reimbursed, except for pre-approved client entertainment, capped at GBP 60 per person.

7. Taxis and private cars
Taxis may be claimed only when no reasonable public transport exists, or for journeys starting after 22:00.
Use of a private car is paid at 45p per mile for the first 10,000 business miles in a tax year and 25p per mile after that.

8. Corporate cards
Corporate card holders have a monthly limit of GBP 3,000. Personal spending on a corporate card is a disciplinary matter.

9. Making a claim
Claims must be submitted through the Ledgerline expenses system within 30 days of the expense.
Claims submitted more than 90 days after the expense will be rejected.
Questions about this policy go to the Finance team at the Coventry head office.
